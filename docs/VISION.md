# GramsevaMitra — Vision (simple overview)

## The problem

Many people in India need to:

- scan and share government or school papers  
- fill forms from photos  
- resize images for portals  
- keep work going even on slow mobile data  

Existing tools are often scattered, hard to trust, or too expensive.

## Our promise

GramsevaMitra is a **friendly all-in-one helper**:

1. Start free — useful tools without payment  
2. Upgrade only when AI “heavy lifting” is needed  
3. Prefer keeping files on the user’s own phone  
4. Feel like a real phone app, not a website shortcut  

## Who it’s for

- Students and job seekers  
- Small business owners  
- Anyone dealing with paperwork on a phone  

## Near-term goals

- Reliable Android app (opens inside the app, not Chrome)  
- Clear Pro upgrade with UPI  
- Contact support at contact@gramsevamitra.com  
- Simple screens that work on small phones and weak networks
